# Commercial imagery — purchase gating (no purchases made)

Rule: **open data first; commercial imagery only for the highest-ranked small
areas, archive before tasking, and only with display/derivative rights.**

## Priority order

1. **WorldView-3 SWIR — first commercial purchase.** ~31 cm pan, ~1.24 m VNIR,
   ~3.7/7.5 m SWIR with material-discrimination bands. The only buy that adds
   site-scale mineral mapping (not just prettier RGB). Buy only after open data
   narrows the area; license must permit public display / derived publication.
2. **Planet Tanager (commercial)** — 400+ bands VSWIR + ISOFIT surface
   reflectance for regional mapping where EnMAP/EMIT/open scenes fall short.
   Limitation: 30 m pixels mix lithologies.
3. **PlanetScope** — near-daily 3 m monitoring (exposure, change, date selection),
   not mineral ID.
4. **VHR optical/stereo** (WorldView, Pléiades Neo) — lineaments, workings,
   access, stereo DEM, traverse planning. 30-cm RGB ≠ mineral identification.
5. **Pixxel Firefly / Wyvern** — VNIR-only hyperspectral (470–900 nm / ≤31 bands):
   iron oxides and surface materials yes; clay/mica/carbonate SWIR features no.

## Pre-purchase checklist (verify every line item)

Wavelength coverage · band count · bandwidth · SNR · surface-reflectance
availability · geolocation accuracy · cloud/shadow masks · public-display and
derived-data rights. Do not buy on the word "hyperspectral" alone.

## Platforms

UP42 (multi-vendor multispectral/hyperspectral/SAR/DEM aggregation); SkyFi
(self-service archive + transparent quotes). Prefer archive imagery over new
tasking whenever possible.

## Open-scene development first

Planet's >150 CC-BY Tanager open scenes and Wyvern 5 m/31-band samples are for
pipeline development and demonstration (coverage of our targets not guaranteed).
EnMAP (420–2450 nm, 30 m, L2A SR) and NASA EMIT L2B mineral abundances
(10 classes + uncertainties, arid regions) are the free spectral workhorses —
check coverage per target before any commercial conversation.
