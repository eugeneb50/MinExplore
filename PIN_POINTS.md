## Pin types
* 🟠 **out** — bedrock outcrop to map/sample (lithology + structure)
*  **pale** — pale/argillic surface: map extent, identify rock, 100 m chip grid, hunt blue-green
* 🔴 **mine** — documented SGM work: walk it, photograph, sample walls/chips
* 🩷 **struct** — fault/lineament: trace, measure, sample gouge/scarp
* 🟢 **sed** — sediment pair (2 bags) for Au/Ag/Cu/Pb/Zn/Mo ICP-MS
* ✚ **TARGET** — your survey pin (reference)

## How to use
1. Load `PIN_POINTS.csv` into a phone GPS app (or export to GPX on request).
2. Take the matching PNG to the truck (each sheet = one field stop cluster).
3. At each pin: record GPS, 1 north-facing photo, samples per type.
4. Expected-accuracy: imagery geolocation ±10–20 m, phone GPS ±5–15 m → allow 15–30 m search radius around each pin (the numbered circle is ~30 m across).

## Notes & caveats
* **Imagery:** Esri World Imagery (Maxar-class, ~2024–2026) via the public tile service — for **research/personal use**; do not republish. S2 2020 panels are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
* **Old works are small** (catas 2–5 m, drifts, 5–50 m pits) — at 1 m/px they are a few pixels; expect to *find* them on the ground within ~100 m of each mine pin using the SGM descriptions (e.g., La Prieta: "open pit, NE trend"; Esperanza: "trenches N65–75°E").
* Zones B/C use the open S2 2020 source (no sub-meter service needed at their distances); re-run the script with `provider: "esri"` for those windows if you want sub-meter there too.
